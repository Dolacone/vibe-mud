## Problem Statement

Players can collect and convert materials into typed Resources, but no Action consumes Resources to create processed Items.

## Recommended Direction

Add one backend-owned Wood Component recipe at Camp. The frontend selects the recipe by identifier. The backend atomically applies its AP cost, Resource cost, and Item output.

## Key Assumptions

- The first recipe always succeeds when its requirements are met.
- Success probability remains outside this change.
- Existing Inventory storage can hold Wood Component quantities.

## Acceptance Criteria

REQ-009 is the source of truth. The plan stage will copy each criterion into its owning task.

## MVP Scope / Not Doing

- No random crafting outcomes.
- No crafting skill modifiers.
- No building modifiers or requirements.
- No Item weight, durability, or use behavior.
- No batch crafting.
